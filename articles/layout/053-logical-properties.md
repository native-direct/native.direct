# Logical properties: margin-inline instead of margin-left

**Reader question:** Why use margin-inline instead of margin-left?

## Outline

- **Problem:** left and right styles break in right-to-left and vertical languages.
- **Native solution:** inline and block properties follow the writing direction automatically.
- **Link out:** MDN logical properties guide.
